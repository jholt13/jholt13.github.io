Hi there!

I'm Justin, thanks for taking a moment to stop by!  This is my first earnest attempt at blogging in who knows how long, but now seems like a good time.  I feel like I recently [reached the end of the internet](https://hmpg.net) and instead of doomscrolling I'd like to put something out onto the internet that some folks might find helpful.

I've been part of the Mac Admins community since 2015 and its been integral to the success I've had in my career.  So many people have been willing to share knowledge and tools that have given me the opportunity to be better.  My hope is that someone stumbles across something I post here and it helps them, like so many before have done for me.

Here's some of the things I'm hoping to write about:
* Behind the scenes of our MDM migration
* Opinionated guides on how I manage different systems
* Quirky issues that I run into that I wish someone else had already documented

If you want to connect, feel free to reach out to me on [Mastodon](https://mas.to/@jholt) or the MacAdmins Slack where I'm `@justinholt`.

See ya soon.
